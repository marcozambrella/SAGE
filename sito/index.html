The problem

Living alone with Alzheimer's is full of small risks

Families want their loved one to stay at home as long as possible, but nobody can be there all day. The dangers are quiet ones, and they are easy to miss from far away.

Forgotten or doubled pills

Memory loss makes it hard to know whether today's dose was already taken.

Not drinking, not eating

Thirst and appetite fade with age and dementia; dehydration creeps in unnoticed.

Falls, nights and wandering

A fall when nobody is around, restless nights, or the front door opening at 3 a.m.

About 57 million people were living with dementia worldwide in 2021, according to the World Health Organization; Alzheimer's disease is the most common form.

How it works

From the cameras at home to a calm message on your phone

  1. 1

    Cameras at home

    Ordinary IP cameras, or an old phone, in the kitchen, the living room, the hallway and the bedroom.

  2. 2

    Models on the home computer

    Object detection, body pose and tracking run on a computer in the house. Small analysers recognise a drink, a pill, a meal, a fall, a night-time rise or an exit.

  3. 3

    One event log

    Every camera speaks the same language. Events seen twice are merged, then turned into daily, weekly and monthly reports.

  4. 4

    Web app and Telegram

    The family reads the day in the web app. Falls and night-time exits arrive at once on Telegram.

Features

Everything the family asked us to watch

Medication, on time

Recognises the pill box and the blister from photos you upload, and tells you if a dose was on time, late or missed.

Water in millilitres

Measures how far the level drops in their usual glass or bottle: 30% of a 1,000 ml bottle is 300 ml.

Meals

What was on the plate, and how much of it was eaten.

Falls

An instant Telegram alert with a photo, so someone can call or go round straight away.

Nights

How many times they got up, for how long, and which rooms they walked through.

Exits at the wrong time

An alert if the front door is used during the hours you choose. Pause it or switch it off from Telegram.

Reports

Daily, weekly and monthly summaries, calm enough to read in a minute and to bring to the doctor.

Trends

Hydration, medication, meals and nights over weeks and months, to notice slow changes early.

Privacy

The videos never leave the home

  • All video is processed on the computer in the house. It is never uploaded or stored online.
  • Only short event records leave the home ("drank 200 ml at 10:30"), with one small still photo when an alert needs it.
  • No face recognition. The family's setup is kept in their own browser, with no account.
